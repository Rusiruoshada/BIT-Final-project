Instructions for Package Selection

🎯 Select Your Vehicle Categories

Please select the vehicle categories you wish to enroll in. You may select one or more categories based on your training goals.

  • Available Categories: Bike, Three-Wheel, Car (Manual/Auto), Light Vehicle and Heavy Vehicles
  • Automatic Bundling: If you select Bike, Three-Wheel, and Car simultaneously, the system will automatically select Light Vehicle for you.
  • Heavy Vehicles: Enrolling in a heavy vehicle requires a valid light vehicle driving license and a minimum of 2 years of driving experience, subject to verification.
💰 Pricing & Lesson Guidelines
  • Registration Fee: A one-time fee of Rs. 5,000.00 applies regardless of the number of categories selected.
  • Standard Lesson Durations & Rates:
    • Bike & Three-Wheel: Rs. 1,000.00 / lesson (30 minutes per lesson)
    • Car (Manual / Auto): Rs. 1,500.00 / lesson (30 minutes per lesson)
    • Light Vehicle: Standard package rates apply based on selections.
    • Heavy Vehicle: Rs. 2,000.00 / lesson (45 minutes per lesson)
  • Custom Lesson Packages:
    • You can customize your lesson counts for any category.
    • Minimum Lesson Requirements: Car require a minimum of 5 lessons. Bike / Three-Wheel minimum of 1 lesson. Heavy vehicle categories require a minimum of 6 lessons.
    • You are free to add more lessons as needed; the total price will update automatically. Lesson counts cannot be reduced below the specified minimums.
